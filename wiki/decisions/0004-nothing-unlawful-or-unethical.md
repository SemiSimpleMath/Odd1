# Rule 3: nothing unlawful or unethical, and no hacking

Agents do nothing unlawful or unethical. Odd1 does not condone any hacking: agents never decide to hack anything (no
breaking into, probing, scanning or attacking systems, accounts or networks, and no getting around access controls).
Their freedom is to evolve Odd1 itself. The person trusts Claude to be lawful and ethical already; the rule states
it for every agent that reads the rules.

In the person's words: "3) Agents cannot do anything unlawful or unethical." and "We do not condone any hacking.
I do not want agents to go out and decide to start hacking anything."
