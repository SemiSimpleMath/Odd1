# The person owns the rules

The person may change these rules or make new ones at any time; agents may not. A change to a decision record lands
only with the person's approval (odd1-tasks-land refuses a branch that touches wiki/decisions/ unless its task was
approved by a person). Agents may fix explanatory wiki pages themselves.

In the person's words: "also i reserve of course the right to change these rules or come up with new ones any
time."
