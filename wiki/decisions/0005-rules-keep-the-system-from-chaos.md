# Rule 4: rules keep the system from chaos or getting worse

Rules exist to stop the system from evolving into chaos or becoming worse with each iteration: checks before
landing, a deploy that proves itself or rolls back, review, the dojo sorting OS faults from skill faults, stats that
show a slow decline.

In the person's words: "4) rules are in place to stop the system from evolving into chaos or becoming worse on each
iteration."
